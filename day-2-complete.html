Kate

"Naka-relate ko ani na butang kay nahitabo kanamo kadaghan. Si Justin, permi ko mag-negative ug hunahuna sa iyang lihok — sama sa pag-hilom kaayo niya, dili kaayo siya makig-estorya kanako. Ako dayong hunahunaan ug negative na mga butang. Dapat dili ko mag-hunahuna ug negative kaniya — akong hunahunaon is nagkinahanglan lang siya og support ug comfort gikan kanako."

"I can relate to this topic because it happens a lot with Justin. When he goes very quiet and doesn't really talk to me, I tend to immediately think negative thoughts. I should not be thinking negatively about him — what I should think is that he just needs support and comfort from me."

A note for Kate

Kate, what you noticed about yourself today is something most people never catch. When Justin goes quiet, the easy thing is to fill that silence with fear — to decide it means something is wrong, or that he is pulling away. You felt that pull, and then you stopped yourself and asked a better question: what does he need from me? That reframe — from judgment to curiosity, from "what's wrong" to "how can I support him" — is one of the most loving things a partner can do. Justin is lucky to be with someone who fights for the generous interpretation, even when it doesn't come naturally.

Justin

"This morning while I was looking after Austin I tend to look at my wife negatively while she is sleeping, but I need to understand that she wakes up during the night and is very tired. This morning I have seen her put Austin back to sleep, play with Austin, and continue to do laundry. I liked when she said, 'this is what we will do today' and planned ahead in the day. I have communicated I want to see more of that and she has improved. I know my loving wife has good intentions always and I should always see her that way."

"Karong buntag, samtang nag-atiman ko kang Austin, namatikdan nako nga nagtan-aw ko sa akong asawa ug negatibo samtang natulog siya — apan kinahanglan nako masabtan nga magmata siya sa gabii ug hilabihan kaayo ang iyang kapoy. Karong buntag nakita nako siya nga gipatulog balik si Austin, naglaro kang Austin, ug nagpadayon sa paglaba. Nalipay ko niadtong miingon siya, 'Mao kini ang atong buhaton karong adlaw' ug nagplano sayo sa adlaw. Gisultihan ko siya nga gusto nako makita pa niana ug nag-improve na siya. Nahibal-an ko nga ang akong hinigugmang asawa adunay maayong tuyo kanunay ug kinahanglan nako siyang makita sa ingon."

A note for Justin

Justin, you caught yourself mid-thought — and that is genuinely rare. Most people don't notice the negative lens until it's already shaped their mood and their words. You noticed it, and then you deliberately looked for evidence of the opposite: Kate putting Austin back to sleep, playing with him, doing laundry. You built your own case for the positive perspective in real time. Ending with "I know my loving wife has good intentions always and I should always see her that way" is not just a nice thought — it's a commitment. Kate feels the difference when you see her that way. Keep going.
