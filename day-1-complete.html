What Is Positive Perspective?

December 12, 2023
✓ Day 1 Complete
Your Responses
Kate

"Ang among relasyon ni Justin kalma ug tinabangay — dili lang isip partners o parents, isip team. Buhaton namo tanan para ma-organise ug planado kami, dili mag-away sa mga dili masabtan. Kay gusto ni Justin nga tinabangay mi ug fair sa usag usa, mao nang dili mi magkagulo. Nagmahigugmaon mi dili para sa amo kaugalingon, kondili para kang Austin — aron mahatagan namo siya og maayong pamilya. Ang among positibo naggikan sa amo pag-estoryahanay. Kung naay away, amo kini ginaestoryahan dayon — dili ipa-ugma. Honest mi sa usag usa. Mao kana ang nagpapositive sa amo pag-uban."

"Our relationship with Justin is calm and built on teamwork — not just as partners or parents, but as a team. We do everything to stay organised and planned so neither of us gets overwhelmed and we don't fight over misunderstandings. Justin is the reason we don't fight — because he wants us to work together and be fair with each other. We love each other not just for ourselves but for Austin — so we can give him a good family. The reason our relationship is more positive is because we talk things through. When there's a conflict, we sort it out straight away — we don't let it carry over. We're honest with each other so we can keep improving. That honesty is what makes our time together more positive."

A note for Kate

Kate, what you wrote is quietly remarkable. You see your relationship clearly — not through rose-coloured glasses, but through the lens of real commitment. The fact that you name teamwork, fairness, and talking things through as the foundation of your positivity shows how deeply you understand what makes love last. The way you centre Austin in this — choosing to build something good for him — speaks to the kind of mother and partner you are. Justin is lucky to be loved by someone who pays attention to the small things that hold a relationship together.

Justin

"Sometimes when Kate wakes up and looks so cranky, I need to understand that she isn't really a morning person. I will admit when she complains I get a bit dysregulated — but I need to understand that she has a lot going on in her mind. She does a lot, and the biggest one is looking after Austin. It takes a lot to look after him, especially when I am at work, and I am very appreciative. I know Kate always tries, and I am very blessed to have her. She has improved and adjusted for me, and she should always be seen in a positive perspective."

"Usahay kung magmata si Kate ug tan-awon kaayong grabe ang iyang pagkasubo, kinahanglan nako masabtan nga dili siya tinuod nga morning person. Moangkon ko nga kung mag-reklamo siya, morag mabalda ko gamay — apan kinahanglan nako masabtan nga daghan kaayo ang naa sa iyang hunahuna. Daghan kaayo ang iyang ginabuhat, ug ang pinakadako niini mao ang pag-atiman kang Austin. Daghan kaayo ang paningkamot sa pag-atiman kaniya, labi na kung naa ko sa trabaho, ug dako kaayo akong pagpahalipay. Nahibal-an ko nga pirmi nagtingkamot si Kate, ug panalangin ko nga naa siya kanako. Nag-improve siya ug nag-adjust alang kanako, ug kinahanglan siyang makita pirmi sa positibong panan-aw."

A note for Justin

Justin, this is exactly what positive perspective looks like in everyday life — and you described it perfectly. The fact that you can catch yourself getting dysregulated and then consciously choose understanding over reaction? That takes real self-awareness and real love. Recognising how much Kate carries, especially while you're at work, and naming her as someone who always tries — that is a husband who truly sees his wife. Kate feels that. Keep choosing that perspective. It is one of the most powerful things you bring to your relationship.
